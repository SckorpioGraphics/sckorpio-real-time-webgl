SCKORPIO GRAPHICS — REAL-TIME WEBGL
===================================

This repository continues the WebGL Fundamentals curriculum with Chapters
21–27. The foundation chapters 0–20 are maintained separately at:
https://github.com/SckorpioGraphics/sckorpio-graphics-webgl

PHASE 4 — ANIMATION AND SPRITE SHEETS
Chapters 21–25: The render loop, delta time, clock objects, transform
animation, animated color and alpha, texture scrolling, and sprite-sheet
playback.

PHASE 5 — MOVEMENT AND BASIC PHYSICS
Chapter 26: Two-dimensional velocity, acceleration, gravity, projectile
motion, and a bow-and-arrow simulation.

PHASE 6 — COLLISION DETECTION AND RESPONSE
Chapter 27: Object bounds, AABB collision detection, and collision responses
including stopping and exchanging velocities.

RUNNING A LESSON
----------------
Serve the repository root over HTTP:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

Open http://localhost:8000. index.html loads Chapter 27j by default; change
its module path to run another lesson. Lil GUI and gl-matrix are loaded from
CDNs declared in index.html.
