SCKORPIO GRAPHICS — REAL-TIME WEBGL
===================================

This repository currently contains Chapters 0–11 plus the later circular and
collision-response lesson directories Chapters 13 and 14. The introductory
foundation curriculum through Chapter 20 is maintained separately at:
https://github.com/SckorpioGraphics/sckorpio-graphics-webgl

PHASE 1 — TIME AND ANIMATION
Chapter 0: requestAnimationFrame(), elapsed time, delta time, FPS, and clocks.
Chapter 1: animated vertex color.
Chapters 2–5: transform animation, animated color and alpha, texture scrolling,
and sprite-sheet playback.

PHASE 2 — LINEAR AND GRAVITY MOTION
Chapters 6–9: velocity, acceleration, unit-vector movement, friction, gravity,
projectiles, tanks, missiles, airplane-style motion, chase cameras, and
bow-and-arrow demonstrations.

PHASE 3 — ROTATIONAL AND COMBINED MOTION
Chapter 10: angular velocity and coupled linear/rotational motion for wheels,
bikes, and tanks.

PHASE 4 — AXIS-ALIGNED COLLISION DETECTION
Chapter 11: object bounds, local and world AABBs, complex-mesh bounds, multiple
objects, collision visualization, penetration correction, textures, vehicle
controls, and parking-car collision scenes.

PHASE 5 — CIRCULAR COLLISION EXPERIMENTS
Chapter 13: circular meshes, circular bounding boxes, circle-circle collision
detection, freeze-on-collision, velocity exchange, and sticking behavior.

PHASE 6 — COLLISION RESPONSE
Chapter 14: exchanging velocities after collisions with both regular and
single-check response strategies.

EXPERIMENTAL EXAMPLES
chapterExtra contains additional tank, projectile, thrust, bow-and-arrow, and
parking-challenge experiments that extend the main chapter sequence.

RUNNING A LESSON
----------------
Serve the repository root over HTTP:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

Open http://localhost:8000. index.html loads Chapter 11o by default; change
its module path to run another lesson. Lil GUI and gl-matrix are loaded from
CDNs declared in index.html.
